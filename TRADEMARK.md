# Name and trademark policy

The BiteCheck **code** is free software under the GPL-3.0. You may fork it, change it and distribute it under the same license.

The **name** "BiteCheck" and the BiteCheck logo identify the official project and its registry. To avoid confusing players about which launcher and registry they can trust:

- Forks and modified versions must use a different name and must not present themselves as the official BiteCheck.
- You may say that your project is "based on BiteCheck" or "a fork of BiteCheck".
- Official builds are signed with the project's key. Only those are official BiteCheck releases.

Using the name to refer to the project (in articles, videos, mod descriptions such as "verified by BiteCheck" for registry-approved mods) is welcome.
